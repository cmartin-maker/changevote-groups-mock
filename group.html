Measures · 3 positions
YES
Prop C — Renter Stabilization Fund ✓ Support
City ballot measure · June 2026
Creates a $40M annual fund for emergency rental assistance, tenant legal defense, and anti-harassment enforcement. This is one of the most important tenant protections on any SF ballot in a decade. Vote YES.
NO
Prop D — Developer Streamlining Act ✕ Oppose
City ballot measure · June 2026
Despite its name, Prop D would gut discretionary review rights that tenants and neighbors use to challenge projects that displace existing communities. The "streamlining" language obscures real harm to anti-displacement tools. Vote NO.
YES
Prop E — Tenant Right to Counsel ✓ Support
City ballot measure · June 2026
Guarantees free legal representation to any SF renter facing eviction. Cities with right-to-counsel programs see eviction rates drop by over 50%. This saves homes. Vote YES.

About us
The SF Tenants Union has been fighting for renters' rights in San Francisco since 1970. We are a member-supported, non-partisan organization that provides free counseling, legal referrals, and political advocacy to tenants across all SF neighborhoods.
Every election cycle we publish an independent voter guide based solely on candidate records and responses to our questionnaire — no political donations accepted, no endorsements for sale. Our positions are voted on by the full membership.
On Change.vote, our official account posts group endorsements and positions. Individual member posts reflect their own views and are labeled accordingly.
Focus areas
🏠 Rent control ⚖️ Just cause eviction 🚫 Ellis Act reform 🏛 Tenant legal defense 🏗 Affordable housing 👴 Senior housing 🔑 SRO protections 📜 Anti-harassment policy
How we make endorsements
1
Candidate questionnaire
We send a detailed written questionnaire to every candidate in races that affect SF tenants. Questions cover rent control, Ellis Act, just cause, and housing supply. All responses are published in full — you can read them on our website.
2
Candidate Q&A sessions
We invite all candidates to a live Q&A hosted on Change.vote where members can ask follow-up questions and hear candidates respond in their own words. Attendance (or non-attendance) is part of our record.
3
Member vote
Our steering committee presents recommendations to the full membership. Endorsements require a majority vote of members present at our general meeting. Any member can speak for or against a candidate during deliberations.
4
Published with reasoning
Every endorsement and opposition comes with a written explanation of our reasoning. We update positions if a candidate's record or public statements change materially before election day.
